## 3. Rules fixed before any P8 fit

- **Markets.** `h2h` only (D-50).
- **Scope.** MLB only, with no NFL arm (D-51).
- **The comparator.** A GBM in two pre-registered variants: a public-only primary and a market-anchored diagnostic (D-50).
- **De-vigging.** Shin, through the project's own `uniroot` solver, is primary. Power and multiplicative are mandatory sensitivities, and the conclusion must hold under all three (D-47).
- **Exchange quotes.** A quote whose book sum is at or below 1 gets `status = "no_overround"` and `z = NA`. It is dropped from the de-vigged benchmark, counted in `out/p8/devig_dropped.csv`, and never normalised (D-68, UT-23).
- **SBRO `NL` rows.** A game whose `Open` or `Close` is the literal `NL` had no line. It is dropped listwise from every de-vigged benchmark and every persistence test, and the dropped count is reported per season in `out/p8/sbro_dropped.csv` (D-68, UT-24).
- **Panels.** The paid panel is 2025 and 2026. Persistence rests on SBRO 2010–2021. The two panels are different objects and are never pooled (D-45).
- **Kalshi data.** Pulled for analysis and kept raw, private and gitignored. Only binned aggregates are published (D-46).
- **Return on investment.** One descriptive line at the actual closing price, never in a heading, abstract or figure, and marked as not a strategy. No stake sizing, no Kelly and no bankroll curve (D-49).

The tests that check these rules, SOP section 6, verbatim:

> **UT-23 `shin_probs` returns `status = "no_overround"` and `z = NA` for `odds = c(2.10, 2.10)` and for any decimal pair with `sum(1/odds) ≤ 1`, and `status = "ok"` with `z > 0` for `c(1.50, 2.70)` at `p[1] = 0.648148 ± 1e-6`**

> **UT-24 SBRO `NL` rows are dropped listwise and the dropped count is returned, not imputed**

> BR-3: the WP surface reaches r ≥ 0.837 and MAE ≤ 0.215 pp against `delta_home_win_exp`, and r ≥ 0.998 against `home_win_exp`; the de-vigged market's own log loss on SBRO 2010–2021 lies in [0.64, 0.70] nats, computed on the post-`NL`-drop denominator.

> WR-17 the P8 ban list and the required "How this differs from rsi2bot" heading naming all three differentiators

> GD-1x reserved for P8's separate, forward-looking market seal.

## 4. The sealed windows

- **2026.** The games of the sealed set in `PREREGISTRATION.md` section 6. This window is a calibration and direction check (D-48). SOP W8.14 scores it once.
- **2027.** A second sealed window over the 2027 regular season, pre-registered here (D-48).

The power statement, SOP W8.13b, verbatim:

> **W8.13b The power addendum, before the seal.** With n ≈ 125 and a plausible `sd(D_i) ≈ 0.10`, the 95% CI half-width is `1.96 × sd(D_i) / sqrt(n) ≈ ±0.018 nats` — larger than any realistic model-versus-market difference. **State plainly that the sealed window cannot resolve a small edge.**

In this annex's words: the 2026 sealed window cannot resolve a small difference between the model and the market.
